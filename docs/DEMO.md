Use only the sample workspace for this walkthrough. The appearances and purchases are fictional.

1. **0–15s:** Load sample venue from Workspace & import. Campaigns opens by default. Keep the deterministic sample baseline selected and draft the invitation queue. The sample venue, shows and purchases are fictional.
2. **15–35s:** Review the two-show queue and invitation preview. Select Review invitation, open Evidence, then Review audience & recipient previews. Inspect the fictional baseline and exclusions.
3. **35–55s:** Mark the top recommendation sold out in the review stage. Open Campaign preferences, replan, and show the changed available recommendation. Edit the copy if desired.
4. **55–75s:** Approve and export CSV or JSON. Messages remain “not sent.” Open the recipient preview and the explicitly simulated checkout; a click does not record a purchase.
5. **75–100s:** Use the separate Operator demo controls to record a sample second purchase at the current demo clock. Open Outcomes: the rate has no mature denominator yet. Advance the fictional clock 30 days to mature follow-up.
6. **100–120s:** Show the frozen cohort, one sample repeat purchase, immutable observation time and disconnected delivery. Explain that this demonstrates measurement, not incremental lift.
